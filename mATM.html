Micro ATM is a small handheld card swipe/Card reader machine with which customers can perform basic banking transactions with the help of a business correspondent or Micro ATM agent from their retail shops.

To become Micro ATM service provider with the medium of MegoPay Mobile Application and to enjoy immense benefits, few requirements need to be fulfilled:

    Download the SaralPe Mobile App from Google Play Store A smartphone with Bluetooth connectivity Sign up and give the KYC details required to complete the registration process. Buy good quality Micro ATM Machine

After fulfilling the above requirements, you are all set to offer services to your customers with an opportunity of earning extra income with limitless offers and schemes.

  • Micro ATM can perform all internal activities related to performing transactions promptly.
  • It maintains certain details of recent transactions for a particular period and all stored data on the device will be stored in an encrypted format.
  • Micro ATM does not transmit any confidential data. Hence it is a safe and secure mode.
  • It automatically logs out the operator and locks itself after completing every transaction
  • Service is available 24*7
  • Instant payment settlement
  • Provide e receipt for the transactions within a few seconds
